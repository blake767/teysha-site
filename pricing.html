Good to Know

A few questions worth answering up front.

Why a setup fee instead of just a monthly price?

The setup fee covers real in-person work — learning your business, writing the call scripts, connecting your calendar, and testing it before it ever answers a real customer. It's not a subscription tool you configure yourself.

What if I'm not very tech-savvy?

That's the point — you shouldn't have to be. I handle the setup and the ongoing management. Your part is answering questions during the setup call and running your business the way you already do.

Is there a contract?

No. The monthly management fee is month to month. If it's not working for you, you can walk away.

Do you serve areas outside DFW?

Not right now — the in-person setup and local relationship is a core part of how this works, so it's DFW-only for the time being.
