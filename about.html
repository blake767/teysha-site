The Name

Where "Teysha" comes from.

Taysha is a Caddo word meaning "friend" or "ally." Spanish explorers spelled it Tejas; English spelling later settled on Texas — the state's name, and its motto, "Friendship," both trace back to it. The Caddo Nation, whose homeland spanned what's now East Texas, is a federally recognized tribe today.

It's the name for this business for two reasons: it's Texas, where I've lived and worked my whole life — and it's the relationship it describes. An ally sits on your side of the table, not across from it. That's the difference between a national AI vendor selling you a subscription, and someone local who sets it up, tunes it, and answers the phone if something goes wrong.
